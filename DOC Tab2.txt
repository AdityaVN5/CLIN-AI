Think of this project as an AI assistant for doctors that automatically creates medical documentation.
The Problem
Today, after seeing a patient, a doctor spends a lot of time writing:
Patient symptoms
Observations
Diagnosis
Treatment plan
Medical records
Billing codes
This takes time away from patient care.

What Your Project Does

Step 3: SOAP Note Agent
The transcript is sent to GPT.
GPT converts the conversation into a medical note.
Input:
Plain Text
Patient reports chest pain since yesterday.
No breathing issues.
Show more lines
Output:
Plain Text
Subjective:
Patient reports chest pain.
 
Objective:
Patient alert and responsive.
 
Assessment:
Possible cardiac chest pain.
 
Plan:
Recommend ECG and blood work.
Show more lines
This saves the doctor from manually writing notes.

Step 4: Compliance Agent
Checks whether the SOAP note is complete.
It asks:
✅ Is Subjective present?
✅ Is Objective present?
✅ Is Assessment present?
✅ Is Plan present?

What You Can Say in an Interview
"I developed an AI Clinical Documentation Assistant using Streamlit, Python, Whisper, and Azure OpenAI. The solution converts doctor-patient conversations into structured SOAP notes, validates documentation completeness, recommends ICD-10 codes, and generates EHR-ready reports in PDF, DOCX, and JSON formats. The objective is to reduce physician documentation effort and improve clinical workflow efficiency."
